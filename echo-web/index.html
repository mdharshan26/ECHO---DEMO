The Problem Statement

In college canteens, hospitals, and service centers, wait times suddenly spike due to:

  • Sudden rush of people (lunch surge / emergency shift)
  • Staff shortages or unplanned counter operator absence
  • Slower service latency at specific bottleneck counters
Flaws in Existing Systems

Existing queue systems only display current queue length or static estimated waiting times.

Critical Gap: They do not help administrators decide what action to take when conditions change!
The ECHO Solution

ECHO bridges the decision gap by predicting waiting times, detecting disruptions, and simulating solutions before execution.

  • Simulates opening standby counters
  • Simulates redirecting traffic to less crowded counters
  • One-click automated decision execution
